# Inventory Procedures:

## Receiving:
1. Pull packing slip from order.
2. Verify all parts on packing slip are present and accounted for.
3. Circle all received quantities on packing slip.
4. Put parts away on shelf.
5. Scan packing list, email to Haley, CC Ted to let her know the order has been received.
6. Turn packing list in to Ted.
