## Sample work scopes:

### ACB replacement
MAIN AIR CIRCUIT BREAKER REPLACEMENT SERIES ___* FRAME SIZE ___**
Travel to site, coordinate with site staff, ensure equipment is disconnected/de-energized as appropriate. Remove dead front panels, remove one ___[^1] ___[^2] air circuit breaker (incl. R&I arc chutes, R&R internal accessories as applicable), replace with one new air circuit breaker of like or compatible specification.  Inspect for further damages and report to customer if found, and return equipment to condition found at start of work.  Breaker is delivered and installed at minimum trip settings, customer is responsible for any programming or trip setting adjustment of the breaker. 

* ACB series, (ex. 3WL, 3WA)
** ACB frame size (ex. FS1, FS2, FS3)
