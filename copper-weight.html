How the weight is calculated

Uses the same calculation as the original copper bar worksheet:

  1. Convert each dimension from inches to centimeters: multiply by 2.54.
  2. Multiply length × width × thickness to get volume in cm³.
  3. Multiply volume by copper density, 8.96 g/cm³, to get grams.
  4. Divide grams by 453.592 for pounds, or by 1,000 for kilograms.
  5. Multiply by the number of identical bars for the total.

Results are displayed to two decimal places. A positive weight below 0.01 is shown as <0.01.
