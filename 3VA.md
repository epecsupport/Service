## 3VA MCCB Information

The 5xx and 8xx series ETU do not have a separate control power source like the main ACB, and the trip unit requires 20% In for the internal CTs to power its display.  When these are on a grounding transformer circuit, the breaker doesn't see anywhere near that current unless there's an issue, which inhibits configuring.  Siemens makes a power/test box for this, known as a TD300 that plugs into the socket on the front of the trip unit and provides battery power.
