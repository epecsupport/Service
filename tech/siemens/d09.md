## IMPORTANT INFORMATION ON D09 BREAKERS

The "Z" code on the end of a 3WA breaker indicates additional build options not covered by the catalogue number structure.  Some examples are: \
3WA2340-5AK02-0AA0Z,D09\
3WA2216-3AK02-0AA0Z,D0980/D09D80

When the 3WA breaker was released, it featured a narrower connection stab design that was not compatible with existing bus configurations for the legacy 3WL breakers.  To compensate for this, the D09 option was made available.  D09 breakers use a thicker stab design that interchanges freely with a bus designed for a 3WL breaker.  QT switched to this design to eliminate having two different types of wing brackets to connect the breaker to the bus, but as of summer 2026, D09 availability had become a serious issue.  

Therefore, when replacing any switchboard originally equipped with a D09 breaker, an adapter kit MUST be included to make the breaker compatible with the existing bus.  That kit catalogue numbers and components are listed below: 

**4000A: [QTKC-0056](/Service/library/QTKC-0056_Rev00.pdf)** *includes the following:*\
6 ea. QTIS-0015C\
6 ea. QTIS-0015D\
6 ea. QT-00936B\
12 ea. 1/2-13 x 2.25" bolts\
12 ea. 1/2-13 x 1.5" bolts\
48 ea. 1/2" flat washers\
24 ea. 1/2" lock washers\
24 ea. 1/2-13 hex nuts\
